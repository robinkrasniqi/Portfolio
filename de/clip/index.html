CLIPBOARD
Signature tablet, rethought.

Signotec works closely with clients across a wide range of industries, each with different requirements for their signature workflow. A signature tablet should therefore be flexible enough to support multiple use cases, while sensors and scanners can be optionally installed depending on the specific needs of each customer. This modular approach keeps the platform adaptable, efficient, and scalable without adding unnecessary hardware to every unit.

Signing an agreement also depends on trust and comfort. The person signing should have enough space and time to read, decide, and respond without feeling constrained by a fixed setup.

01 / Concept development

A tablet shaped around the signing experience

Early sketches explore different forms and ways to hold, position, and use the device. The concept aims to give the signer more control over their space while keeping the tablet adaptable to different hardware configurations.

02 / Modular hardware

One platform, different configurations.

The tablet is designed as a base platform that can accommodate optional components. Depending on the use case, features such as camera verification or contactless payment can be included or left out instead of being built into every unit.

Parametric exploration helped develop the form and investigate how components could fit within the product.

03 / Final design

A more comfortable way to sign

A considered shape and flexible setup give the signer a more comfortable, independent interaction while supporting the practical needs of the business.
