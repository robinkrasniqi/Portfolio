Student project

MID TYPE H

The goal of this project was to become familiar with a virtual reality workflow. The Meta Quest 3 was used for for both modeling and visualization. I chose to reimagine the Nissan MID4 for a contemporary audience. A car is an ideal subject for exploring virtual reality, and the MID4 is a fascinating design that I have wanted to explore for some time.

Surfacing 3D sketching in VR

01 / Design exploration

Finding the shape

The Nissan MID4 was a Japanese supercar that never reached production. It was one of several unrealized visions inspired by European mid-engined sports cars. The MID-II's dynamic, organically futuristic design set it apart from other Japanese cars.

The MID4-II has a distinctive side profile. Its beltline, shoulder line, and vent line align to create a dynamic, ribbon-like shape within the A-line. Together with its soft volumes and subtle dome hood, these details are essential to the MID's identity, which I wanted to preserve.

02 / 3D exploration

Sketching at full scale

Gravity Sketch brings sketches into 3D space, where I can work at full scale and explore proportions in virtual reality. Subdivision modeling makes it quick to iterate. The car went through several changes before I brought the model into Alias as a reference for more refined surfacing.
