KONSTRUKTIER

How it works.

Konstruktier is a wooden construction game for children aged four and up. It playfully introduces them to the principles of building physics. Using a limited set of components, players build a portable structure while learning about load-bearing walls and how forces are distributed within a building.

Challenge cards add variety: each card shows a sequence of colors that determines the order in which the floor tiles can be used. Animals appear between levels to complicate the construction process. The Giraffe is enormous and needs two stories of space. The Monkey clings to the facade and makes the building wobble. The Bear pushes upward, forcing the floor above it to tilt.
