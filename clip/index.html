Student project

CLIPBOARD

Signotec works closely with clients across a wide range of industries, each with different requirements for their signature workflow. My goal was to develop a configurable platform that can be individualized with minimal setup.

Signing an agreement also depends on trust and comfort. The person signing should have enough space and time to read, decide, and respond without feeling constrained by a fixed setup.

Surface Design Product development

01 / Concept development

A tablet shaped around the signing experience

Early sketches explore different forms and ways to hold, position, and use the device. The concept aims to give the signer more control over their space while keeping the tablet adaptable to different hardware configurations.

02 / 3D process

Building the Sketch

After sketching, checking proportions, and planning how the parts would be made using injection moulding, I was ready to build the surfaces. I then developed the grip design parametrically in Dynamo. In Inventor, I built the solid model, fitted the components, and refined the shell.

03 / Modular hardware

One product, different configurations.

The tablet is designed as a base platform that can accommodate optional components. Depending on the use case and the client, features such as fingerprint verification, contactless payment, or a card reader can be included or left out.

The cover panel that makes this possible also provides a surface area for branding.
